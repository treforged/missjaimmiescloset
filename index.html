The Story

Meet MissJaimmie

MissJaimmie's Closet was born from a love of fashion, great finds, and giving items a second life. As an Army Veteran and University of Florida graduate (Go Gators! 🐊), Jaimmie brings the same dedication and discipline to curating her closet as she did in service. With 2,000+ active listings and a loyal community of 66,000+ Poshmark followers, MissJaimmie's Closet has become one of Gainesville's most-loved online boutiques.
